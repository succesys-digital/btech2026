# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static single-file HTML with inline CSS and minimal JS (set by the user's brief). Only external resource: Google Fonts (Poppins).

## Users

Two audiences (confirmed by the user, 2026-11-10):
- Independent professionals (brokers, consultants, doctors, lawyers) buying one card for themselves.
- Companies ordering cards for a team, with the company logo.

Situation: meeting someone in person and wanting the contact saved without typing.

## Product Purpose

B-tech sells smart business cards with NFC and QR Code. Tap the card on a phone and the contact is saved; a digital profile opens. No app needed. Tagline: "Encostou, salvou."

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Card size 85.6 x 54 mm. Front: client logo centered. Back: QR Code top-left, B-TECH logo top-right, name bottom-left. Always show this real layout, never a generic card.
- Digital profile included; the customer updates it with login and password, no reprint needed.
- Works on iPhone and Android.
- Lead time: up to 10 business days after artwork approval.
- Price: "a partir de R$ 249 por cartão" (from the brand guide; the current page is a demo and labels it as such).

## Brand Commitments

Source: identity v1 navy guide (`brand-btech-v2/_source/guia-v1.pdf`, October 2026). The client asked to return to it on 2026-11-10 after trying v2 (bordô & marfim).
- Logo files only (`brand-btech-v2/_source/logo-btech-v1-navy.svg` on light, `logo-btech-v1-offwhite.svg` on navy or black); never redraw, recolor, shadow or gradient; min 96px wide. Running text writes "B-tech".
- Colors: navy #0D2540 (header, hero, pricing band, footer, headings, text), off-white #F4EFEA (page background, text on navy), cinza #4A5261 (secondary text), blush #EBC3C8 (decorative only, never text on light), card black #0B0B0C (card mockups only), WhatsApp green #1B8247 (WhatsApp button only).
- Poppins 400-700 everywhere (tabular numbers on prices).
- Card layout: front = client logo centred; back = QR top-left, B-TECH top-right, name bottom-left.
- Premium direction: one big moment (navy hero with oversized headline and the lit card mockup). No uppercase eyebrow labels, no blush rule above every title, arcs used once (hero stage), no scroll-reveal animations, no middle-dot separators.
- Voice: Brazilian Portuguese, direct and close, "você", short sentences, benefit before technology. CTAs: "Quero meu cartão", "Me chama no WhatsApp".
- Contact: Instagram @btech.card, WhatsApp (13) 99165-7810.

## Evidence on Hand

No real testimonials, customers or metrics exist. The user asked for fictional testimonials in the demo; they must stay visibly labeled as fictional.

## Product Principles

- Benefit first: what the visitor gains before how NFC works.
- Show the real card layout, never a generic one.
- Premium, simple, human: technology without a "tech" look.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, 44px touch targets, visible focus states, alt text on logos.
